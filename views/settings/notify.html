Notifications

Alerts on your phone for the anime and manga on your list (Watching, Rewatching and Planning). Your AniList notifications are under the bell at the top of the app.

Release reminders

AniList only gives air times for anime, and about two weeks ahead are planned each time the app opens. Manga chapters have no dates on AniList.

Changes to your titles

These come from AniList and are checked in the background about every 30 minutes, so they can arrive a little late. They need the background add-on in this build; the bug report in About says whether it is on. What AniList notifies you about is still chosen in AniList Settings.

This phone

If reminders arrive late or not at all, some phones stop apps in the background to save battery. In your phone's settings, set What Next? to unrestricted battery use.
